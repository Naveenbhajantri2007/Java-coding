If you start revising just ONE DSA pattern every day, your interview confidence will look very different in 30 days. Most people don’t get rejected because they’re “bad at coding”.   They get rejected because they can’t explain their thinking. Not just to solve problems, but to: • break problems into steps   • choose the right approach   • analyze time & space   • communicate solutions confidently  Power move for beginners    1) Stop chasing random questions.   2) Start mastering patterns.   3) Focus on *why* an approach works, not just *what* works. Interview truth:   Even if your solution isn’t perfect,   a clear explanation can still get you through. Master patterns, not questions.   One pattern = dozens of problems unlocked. Save this cheatsheet.   Revisit weekly.   Let your fundamentals do the heavy lifting.
